Features

Everything that makes it great.

🕹️

Full Arcade Cabinet Form

Every detail of a classic arcade cabinet — marquee, bezel, controls, side art — all packed into a desk-sized charging stand.

⌚

Watch Face IS the Screen

Your Apple Watch slots into the cabinet bezel so the watch face becomes the game screen. A clever illusion that looks incredible.

🔌

Clean Wire Routing

Built-in channels hide your Apple Watch magnetic charger cable so all you see is the cabinet — no wires, no clutter.

📐

Universal Watch Fit

Fits Apple Watch Series 1 through 10, in 38mm, 40mm, 41mm, 44mm, and 45mm sizes. (Not compatible with Apple Watch Ultra.)

🎨

Multiple Color Options

Available in a range of colors. Choose classic black for a realistic look, or go bold with purple, red, or any available color.

🖨️

3D-Printed Unique

Each piece is printed on demand, making it uniquely yours. The retro gaming aesthetic looks even better in person.
