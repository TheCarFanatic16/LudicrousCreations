Features

Everything that makes it great.

📐

Adjustable Angle

Set your phone to exactly the viewing angle you need — whether for calls, cooking, or watching video. Stays locked in place.

📱

Universal Compatibility

Works with all standard smartphones. Wide enough for modern large-screen phones, stable enough for thinner ones too.

🔌

Cable Management Slot

Built-in cable slot lets you charge your phone in the stand without cable clutter. Keep your desk clean and your phone powered.

🛡️

Non-Slip Base

The base is weighted and designed to stay firmly in place on your desk — no sliding, no tipping, even when tapping the screen.

🧳

Lightweight & Portable

Light enough to toss in a bag and take anywhere — the office, a hotel room, or your kitchen counter for recipe reference.

🎨

Many Color Options

Available in a wide range of colors to match your desk setup. Every piece is made to order just for you.
