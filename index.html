Our History

A printer,
a problem,
and a lot of
curiosity.

It started with a single 3D printer and an endless list of "wouldn't it be nice if…" moments. The founder of Ludicrous Creations — based in Royal Oak, Michigan — turned those moments into a catalog of genuinely useful, cleverly designed gadgets.

Opening on Etsy, the shop quickly gained recognition for its unique combination of form and function. What makes Ludicrous Creations different? Every product comes from lived experience — gadgets tested in real kitchens, real cars, and real lifestyles before they ever reach a customer.

Today, the shop boasts thousands of satisfied customers, multiple color options on every product, and a growing catalog of solutions for life's small (but genuinely annoying) problems.
