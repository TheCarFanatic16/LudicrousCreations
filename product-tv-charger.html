Features

Everything that makes it great.

📺

Watch IS the Screen

The Apple Watch face slots in to become the TV screen — a clever illusion that looks better in person than in photos.

🎮

Fidget Knob

The TV control knob actually spins. It's a functional fidget toy built right into the charging station design.

🔌

Clean Wire Routing

A circular cutout in the back holds your magnetic charger in place, and built-in channels hide the cable completely.

⌚

Universal Watch Fit

Fits all Apple Watch models Series 1 through 10, in 38mm, 40mm, 41mm, 44mm, and 45mm sizes. (Not compatible with Apple Watch Ultra.)

🎨

Many Color Options

Available in a wide range of colors to match your desk setup. Every piece is made to order.

3D-Printed Unique

Each piece is printed on demand, making it uniquely yours. Small natural variations in print are part of the character.
